## Overview

As an undergraduate research assistant, I configured a software-defined radio and a Raspberry Pi
(Ubuntu Linux) with GNU Radio to transmit and receive isolated radio signals. I wrote and tested
automated Python and Bash programs to collect data alongside ground-truth measurements, processed
the spectrogram data into graphs for the research paper, and presented a poster at the NY6
Undergraduate Research Conference at St. Lawrence University (Nov 2025).
