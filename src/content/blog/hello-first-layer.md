In 3D printing, the first layer decides whether the whole print succeeds. Get it right and everything stacks cleanly on top. Get it wrong and you come back to spaghetti.

This site is where I'll write down the fundamentals behind things I build: robots, CAD parts, prints, code, and the occasional Minecraft server. Some posts will be step-by-step tutorials; others, like this one, are just notes.
